Wait, What? Uploader

Wait What Uploader is a small personal desktop tool used by the creator of the YouTube channel Wait, What? (@WaitWhat-How) to upload their own short educational videos to their own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app.
  2. The owner picks a finished video file on their own computer.
  3. The app shows the account name, lets the owner write the title, choose who can view the video, and opt in to comments, duet and stitch, and shows TikTok's music usage declaration.
  4. Only after the owner confirms does the app upload the video through TikTok's Content Posting API.

The app is not offered to the public and does not post anything automatically.
