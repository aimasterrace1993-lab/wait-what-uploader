Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

What the app accesses

When the operator signs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token and the operator's basic profile information (display name and open ID) with the permissions the operator approves (user.info.basic and video.publish).

How it is used

The token and profile information are used only to show which account is connected and to upload videos the operator chooses, at the operator's request.

Storage and sharing

The access token is stored only on the operator's own computer. The app has no server and no database, does not collect data about other people, and does not sell, share or send data to anyone other than TikTok.

Retention and deletion

The operator can delete the stored token from their computer at any time and can revoke the app's access in TikTok under Security and permissions.

Children

The app is not directed to children and collects no information from them.

Contact
